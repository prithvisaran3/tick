# Tick copy deck
Rules: lead with the number or the action. Sentence case. Button verbs: Add, Tick, Link bank, Fix, Move to savings. Never "users", "platform", "leverage". No humor on errors, security, bank problems or anything wrong with money. A wink is fine in empty states and success moments.
Branded terms (max six): **Safe to spend**, **Morning Receipt**, **Caught**, **Landed**, **Payday plan**, **Ask Tick**. Everything else is plain words. The name is "Tick" in sentences; the logo is always lowercase `tick`.

## Errors / bank / security (no humor)
- That didn't go through. Try again, nothing was lost.
- Your Chase link needs a quick sign-in to keep syncing.
- Can't reach Tick right now. Showing your last known numbers.
- Face ID didn't match. Try again.
- You have 7 of 10 bank links left.
- Hold to link a bank. Removing a link doesn't give the slot back.
- Hold to delete everything. This can't be undone.
